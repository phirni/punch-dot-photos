# Paper Punch Editor

Recreates the "star / heart punch" photo trend: one photo + one sheet of paper.

- **Photo panel:** the punched-out paper pieces (cut from the same paper) are stuck on the photo, with soft shadows.
- **Paper panel:** the paper with real holes over the photo, so the photo shows through.

## Run
Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No internet or libraries needed; photos never leave your browser.

## Use
1. Upload a photo and pick an aspect ratio (zoom / shift to frame it).
2. Pick a paper colour (or upload a paper texture). Mottling, grain and gold specks are adjustable.
3. Choose a shape (sparkles, star, heart, dot, flower, snowflake, diamond, or your own PNG) and press **Scatter shapes**.
4. Click a shape in either panel to select it; drag to move. Hole = paper panel, piece = photo panel.
5. Export both panels stacked/side by side, or each separately.
